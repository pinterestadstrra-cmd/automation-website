How this works (simple)

1) Make a card below. One card = one job (for example: morning news).
2) Set the big numbers on the card: how many posts per day, and the posting window. The card spreads the posts itself across the day - you do not set an interval for posting.
3) The worker runs your cards every 30 minutes. A card only posts at one of its spread-out posting times - and only when it finds a photo that really loads. No photo, no post. Draft-only cards save a draft instead of publishing.
4) Nothing posts without a card. No card, no post. That is the rule.

How this works (simple)

1) Add your Bluesky account below (handle + app password). Each card picks which account it posts and replies as.
2) Make a card: post the latest article, promote a link, or reply to comments. Set "run every X minutes" - the card checks replies on every run, and only posts at its spread-out posting times.
3) A reply card answers EVERY comment on YOUR posts - it checks old posts too, from the last few days you set on the card - and fresh comments on hot posts by others (limited per run). Every reply is different, and every reply is written in that card's own log (tap "Logs" on the card).
4) Test a card first: tap "Test" on a card - it writes the article and checks the photo, but publishes nothing.
