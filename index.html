How this works (simple)

How this works (simple)

1) There is NO account to create - no signup, no approval, no review. On Nostr your keypair IS your account: the nsec (private key) controls the identity, the npub (public key) is your public address.
2) Get a keypair in any Nostr app: install Primal, Damus or Amethyst on your phone, create a key (or open Settings → Keys / Security on a key you already have) and copy BOTH the npub and the nsec. This dashboard has no key generator - paste the keys from your Nostr app.
3) Paste them below (npub + nsec). The nsec is saved in this browser and in the NOSTR_ACCOUNTS secret (encrypted - only you can see it). NEVER share your nsec with anyone, and never paste it anywhere else - whoever holds it controls your Nostr identity and can post as you. The list below shows only your name + npub, never the nsec back.
4) Make a card: a hook + photo link + link, published as a signed kind-1 note to public relays (relay.damus.io, relay.nostr.band, nos.lol, relay.snort.social). Each card posts fresh Political / War news with a verified real photo - the same story never goes to Bluesky, Reddit, Tumblr, Threads, Mastodon, Telegram, Nostr and Lemmy on the same day. The photo itself is not uploaded - its public URL goes in the note as its own line, and Nostr apps show it inline; if a story has no photo, the post goes out text-only instead of skipping.
5) Nostr is a protocol, not one app. Your notes appear in every Nostr client that reads those relays (Primal, Damus, Amethyst, Snort and more). There is no algorithm feed - people see notes from keys they follow, so share your npub (your Primal profile link) to grow.
6) Nostr v1 is post-only - cards never reply (reply support was not built). Test a card first: tap "Test" on a card - it writes the post and checks the photo, but publishes nothing. Notes are capped at 10000 characters (hook + photo link + link).
